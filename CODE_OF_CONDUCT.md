# Code of Conduct

## Scope

This code applies to everyone participating in Virgo Terminal issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces, including maintainers.

## Standards

- Treat people with respect. Technical disagreement and constructive criticism are welcome.
- Focus feedback on the work. Respect personal boundaries and get consent before sharing private information.
- Harassment, discrimination, threats, and personal attacks are unacceptable.

## Reporting

Use [GitHub's private reporting options](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) to report abusive content or behavior. Include relevant links and context. Keep sensitive details out of public issues and discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, warn participants, lock conversations, or restrict participation. They consider the severity and context of the behavior and respect the privacy of the people involved.
